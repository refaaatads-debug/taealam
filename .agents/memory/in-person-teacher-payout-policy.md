---
name: In-person teacher payout policy
description: Product rule for how in-person earnings appear in teacher reporting and withdrawals.
---

Unpaid in-person lesson earnings belong in monthly earnings reporting only. They must not enter the teacher's withdrawable balance until the platform manually credits a payout. Keep existing financial ledger rows and withdrawal allocations intact; avoid counting the same in-person amount both as an unpaid lesson earning and as a manual payout.

**Why:** The platform manually pays in-person earnings, as it does online teacher earnings, and must avoid creating a second claim or withdrawal source.

**How to apply:** Keep monthly in-person reporting separate from the withdrawable balance. New withdrawal validation and allocation must accept only the manually credited teacher balance; preserve existing in-person records and allocations.
